Understanding Infertility:
Causes and Treatments

Male Factor

Male Infertility

Male infertility often involves issues with sperm production, quality, or delivery. Common causes include:

  • Low Sperm Count: A lower-than-normal number of sperm.
  • Poor Sperm Motility: Sperm that doesn't move as well as it should.
  • Hormonal Imbalances: Disruptions in hormones that affect sperm production.
  • Testicular Problems: Issues with the testicles that produce and store sperm.
Explore Male Fertility
Female Factor

Female Infertility

Female infertility can result from various factors, from ovulation issues to structural complications:

  • Ovulation Irregularities: Problems with the release of eggs from the ovaries.
  • Fallopian Tube Blockages: Obstructions that prevent sperm from reaching the egg.
  • Endometriosis: A condition where uterine tissue grows outside the uterus.
  • Age & Lifestyle: Fertility declines with age, diet, weight, and stress.
Explore Female Fertility

Diagnosis & Treatment

Infertility diagnosis involves a range of tests, including hormone evaluations, imaging studies, and sperm analysis. Treatments can vary based on the underlying cause.

  • Lifestyle Adjustments: Changes in diet, exercise, and stress management.
  • Medications: To regulate hormones and improve fertility.
  • Assisted Reproductive Techniques: Such as IVF and IUI.

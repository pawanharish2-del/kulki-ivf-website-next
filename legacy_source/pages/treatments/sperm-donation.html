Sperm Donation Program
A Vital Option for Family Building

Key Takeaway

The Sperm Donation program allows couples facing severe male infertility or single women to achieve pregnancy using healthy, rigorously screened donor sperm from certified banks.

Overview

Using Donor Sperm

Donor sperm can be used in conjunction with IUI or IVF for couples facing severe male infertility, or for single women and same-sex couples looking to start a family.

  • Solution for azoospermia (no sperm).
  • Avoids transmitting severe genetic diseases.
  • Supports diverse family structures.
  • High-quality samples from certified banks.
Screening

Rigorous Selection

We work with fully accredited sperm banks that enforce strict medical, genetic, and psychological screening for all donors, ensuring the safety of your future child.

  • Extensive medical history review.
  • In-depth genetic carrier screening.
  • Infectious disease testing.
  • Detailed physical characteristics matching.

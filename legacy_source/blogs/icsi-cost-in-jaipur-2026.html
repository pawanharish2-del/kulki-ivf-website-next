Breaking Down the Cost of ICSI

The total cost of an ICSI cycle is not just a single fee; it encompasses several critical medical phases. Here is what is typically included in a standard ICSI package in Jaipur:

  • Ovarian Stimulation Medications: Hormonal injections to produce multiple eggs.
  • Egg Retrieval Procedure: A minor surgical process performed under sedation.
  • Sperm Preparation & ICSI: The highly specialized laboratory procedure where a single sperm is injected directly into an egg using micromanipulators.
  • Embryo Culture: Growing the fertilized eggs in advanced modular incubators for 3 to 5 days.
  • Embryo Transfer: Placing the healthiest blastocyst back into the uterus.

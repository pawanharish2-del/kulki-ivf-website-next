What to expect during egg freezing?

Home / Blog / Egg Freezing Guide

At a Glance

Egg freezing (Oocyte Cryopreservation) is a safe, proactive step to preserve your fertility. The process takes about 2 to 3 weeks and involves hormone stimulation, ultrasound monitoring, and a minor 15-minute egg retrieval procedure under light sedation.

Whether you are prioritizing your career, focusing on health, or simply haven't met the right partner yet, egg freezing empowers you to take control of your biological clock. The process is straightforward, highly advanced, and safe.

The Step-by-Step Timeline

  • Consultation & Testing (Day 1): We check your ovarian reserve via an AMH blood test and ultrasound.
  • Ovarian Stimulation (Days 2-12): You will take self-administered hormone injections to encourage multiple eggs to mature. Monitoring is done via periodic ultrasounds.
  • The Retrieval (Day 14): A painless, 15-minute procedure performed under light sedation. You can go home the same day.
  • Vitrification: Your eggs are instantly frozen using ultra-rapid cooling technology (Vitrification) in our advanced cleanroom lab, safely preserving them for years.

2. Modular Laboratory Incubators & Quality Standards

Embryonic development requires a highly stable, secure micro-environment. A slight shift in air temperature, moisture, or chemical particulate levels can disrupt growth. To prevent cellular stress, a top-tier laboratory cleanroom is essential.

Our facility operates an ISO-certified Class 10,000 modular laboratory cleanroom. Equipped with positive pressure, HEPA filtration, and VOC carbon scrubbers, this cleanroom environment maintains optimal physiological metrics continuously, protecting embryos throughout the culturing phase.

Clinical Protocol Purpose & Implantation Benefits
HEPA Air Filtration Continuously filters out dust and chemical impurities in embryology.
Laser-Assisted Hatching Preparing the outer embryo shell to support uterine attachment.
Blastocyst Transfer Transferring robust Day-5 embryos for higher clinical success.
Warning on Lab Air: Standard incubators without positive air pressure and cleanroom VOC filtration expose embryos to contamination risk, directly lowering success rates.

The embryology laboratory is often called the "heart" of an IVF clinic, and for good reason. In this controlled environment, eggs are fertilized and embryos are cultured for up to five days before transfer. Any fluctuation in ambient air quality, temperature, or gas concentrations can cause cellular stress to the embryos, potentially arresting their development. This is why we have built a state-of-the-art Class 10,000 modular laboratory cleanroom. By maintaining strict control over environmental variables, we ensure that your embryos have the best possible chance of growing into healthy, viable blastocysts.

4. Advanced Sperm Selection & Micro-Fertilization

We provide a comprehensive range of assisted reproductive options, including IVF, ICSI, IUI, and egg donor cycles. For couples facing male factor challenges, Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI) is performed under high-magnification optics to select and inject a single healthy sperm directly into the egg, bypassing common anatomical barriers to fertilization.

We also provide absolute pricing transparency, delivering clear cost breakdowns during your initial visit. By removing financial stress and unexpected costs, we allow patients to focus fully on their health and family goals.

Advanced sperm selection technologies play a vital role in the success of ICSI procedures. In cases of severe male factor infertility, such as low sperm count (oligospermia) or poor motility (asthenozoospermia), selecting the healthiest sperm is crucial. We utilize advanced laboratory techniques like density gradient washing and microfluidic sorting to isolate sperm with high motility and minimal DNA fragmentation. By selecting the best possible sperm cell for micro-injection, we significantly improve fertilization and blastocyst development rates, offering a clear advantage to couples on their path to parenthood.

Essential Diagnostic Tests for Starting a Family

Before launching into any clinical cycle, our medical team conducts a series of highly targeted screening evaluations to assess the biological potential of both partners. This ensures that no time or resources are wasted on ineffective protocols. Below is a detailed breakdown of the primary diagnostic screening tools we utilize:

Diagnostic Assessment Target Entity / Hormone Checked Clinical Significance for Couples
Anti-Mullerian Hormone (AMH) Blood test evaluating ovarian follicle reserves. Determines egg quantity and predicts ovarian response to stimulation drugs.
Antral Follicle Count (AFC) Transvaginal high-resolution ultrasound. Directly visualizes the number of active, resting follicles on the ovaries.
Semenology (Sperm Analysis) Ejaculate volume, count, motility, morphology. Excludes male factor roadblocks and guides decisions between standard IVF or ICSI.
Hysterosalpingography (HSG) Contrast-dyed X-ray of fallopian tubes. Confirms tubal patency and identifies uterine cavity abnormalities.

Frequently Asked Questions (Starting a Family)

When should a couple seek help for fertility?

We recommend seeking a specialist evaluation if conception has not occurred after one year of regular unprotected intercourse, or after six months for women aged 35 and older. Earlier evaluation is recommended if there is a history of irregular periods, endometriosis, or known male factor concerns.

What is Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)?

IUI is a simple fertility treatment where prepared, washed sperm is placed directly inside the uterus around the time of ovulation. This procedure bypasses the cervix and vaginal tract, allowing a higher concentration of active sperm to reach the fallopian tubes, where fertilization occurs naturally.

How are eggs, sperm, and embryos preserved safely?

We use ultra-rapid vitrification freezing to solidify cells instantly, preventing internal ice crystal formation and ensuring excellent cell survival rates when thawing. This allows patients to preserve their fertility for future family building with confidence.

What is the difference between IVF and ICSI?

In standard IVF, retrieved eggs are placed in a dish with thousands of sperm cells, allowing fertilization to occur naturally. In ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection), a senior embryologist uses specialized micro-needles to select a single, structurally perfect sperm cell and inject it directly into the cytoplasm of a mature egg, ensuring fertilization even in cases of severe male infertility.

How long does the entire IVF process take from start to finish?

A standard IVF cycle takes about 4 to 6 weeks. This includes ovarian stimulation (10 to 12 days), egg retrieval, laboratory fertilization and embryo culture (3 to 5 days), followed by the embryo transfer. A pregnancy test is typically scheduled about 12 to 14 days after the transfer to confirm success.

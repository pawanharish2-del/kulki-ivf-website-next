At a Glance

Yes, IVF is highly successful for women with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS). In fact, women with PCOS often have excellent egg reserves, making them prime candidates for successful IVF outcomes when guided by specialized stimulation protocols at clinics like Kulki IVF in Jaipur.

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is one of the most common endocrine disorders affecting women of reproductive age in Rajasthan and across India. The primary fertility issue caused by PCOS is anovulation-the failure to release an egg regularly. When medications like letrozole or IUI do not yield results, IVF steps in as the definitive solution.

Why IVF is Effective for PCOS

During an IVF cycle, fertility specialists take control of the ovulation process. Through careful administration of hormones, we can safely mature multiple eggs simultaneously. Because women with PCOS generally have a high antral follicle count, they often produce a large number of eggs during IVF, which can lead to multiple high-quality embryos.

The Importance of Specialized Protocols

The main risk for PCOS patients undergoing IVF is Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS). At Kulki IVF, Dr. Asha Sushawat employs advanced antagonist protocols and specific trigger shots to almost completely eliminate the risk of OHSS, ensuring a safe and comfortable journey to parenthood.

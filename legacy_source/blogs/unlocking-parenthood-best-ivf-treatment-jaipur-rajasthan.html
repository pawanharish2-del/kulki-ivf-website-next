Unlocking Parenthood: The IVF Journey Step by Step at Kulki IVF

The best IVF treatment in Jaipur, Rajasthan follows a structured, carefully monitored process. Here is how your parenthood journey unfolds at Kulki IVF:

Phase 1 - Diagnosis & Fertility Workup

Before beginning the best IVF treatment, both partners undergo a comprehensive fertility evaluation. This includes hormonal profiling (FSH, LH, AMH, estradiol), antral follicle count ultrasound, sperm analysis, and uterine assessment. The results allow our specialists to design the most effective IVF protocol for your specific needs.

Phase 2 - Controlled Ovarian Stimulation

The female partner receives a customized hormonal stimulation protocol to encourage the development of multiple follicles. At Kulki IVF, we use the latest GnRH agonist and antagonist protocols to optimize egg yield while minimizing the risk of ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS).

Phase 3 - Egg Retrieval & Sperm Preparation

When follicles reach optimal maturity, eggs are retrieved under sedation via transvaginal ultrasound-guided aspiration. Simultaneously, the male partner's sperm sample is processed in our andrology laboratory to select the highest quality sperm for fertilization.

Phase 4 - Fertilization, Embryo Culture & Selection

Eggs and sperm are combined using conventional IVF or ICSI in our advanced embryology laboratory. Embryo development is monitored continuously for 5 days until blastocyst stage, using time-lapse imaging to select the best quality embryo for transfer - a critical element of the best IVF treatment in Jaipur.

Phase 5 - Embryo Transfer & Support

The top-quality embryo is gently transferred into the uterus. Luteal phase support medications are prescribed to maximize implantation chances. After the two-week wait, a blood pregnancy test confirms the outcome.

Who Should Consider the Best IVF Treatment in Jaipur?

The best IVF treatment in Rajasthan at Kulki IVF is indicated for couples facing:

  • Failed IUI cycles (2 or more attempts)
  • Blocked, damaged, or absent fallopian tubes
  • Severe male factor infertility requiring ICSI
  • Advanced maternal age (35 years and above)
  • Poor ovarian reserve (low AMH)
  • Recurrent miscarriages requiring PGT-A testing
  • Unexplained infertility persisting beyond 2 years
  • Genetic conditions requiring preimplantation genetic diagnosis

2. Setting Modular Standards in Embryology Labs

Delicate embryos are highly sensitive to their environment. Contaminants, fluctuating air quality, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in normal air can stop cell growth before a transfer is performed. A premier hospital must house a secure embryology lab cleanroom to preserve embryo health.

Our Class 10,000 modular laboratory uses advanced positive air pressure, carbon filtration, and HEPA air scrubbers. This system filters out 99.97% of particulate matter, ensuring a stable, safe environment for blastocyst growth and maximizing successful implantation outcomes.

HEPA Air Filtration Continuously filters lab air to protect embryos from VOCs and dust.
Laser-Assisted Hatching Thinning the outer embryo shell to boost success for older patients.
Vitrification (Vitrify) Ultra-rapid cooling for safe egg, sperm, and embryo storage.

IVF Treatments Available at Kulki IVF - Best IVF Hospital for Dausa Patients

As the best IVF hospital serving Dausa, Kulki IVF offers a comprehensive spectrum of fertility treatments:

IVF (In Vitro Fertilization)

Our flagship treatment, IVF, is available to couples with a wide range of fertility diagnoses. Our laboratory's advanced technology - including time-lapse embryo monitoring and vitrification cryopreservation - gives every embryo the optimal environment to develop into a healthy pregnancy.

ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection)

ICSI is the gold standard for treating male factor infertility. Our embryologists directly inject a single carefully selected sperm into each mature egg, dramatically improving fertilization rates for couples with poor sperm quality or quantity.

IUI (Intrauterine Insemination)

A less invasive first-line treatment, IUI involves placing prepared sperm directly into the uterus around ovulation. It is suitable for couples with mild male factor infertility, unexplained infertility, or ovulatory disorders.

Egg Freezing & Embryo Cryopreservation

We offer vitrification-based egg and embryo freezing for fertility preservation, allowing couples to store healthy embryos for future use with excellent post-thaw survival rates.

Donor Egg & Donor Sperm IVF

For patients requiring third-party reproduction, Kulki IVF maintains a carefully screened donor programme that strictly follows ICMR guidelines, giving couples additional options on their path to parenthood.

Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT)

We offer PGT-A and PGT-M testing to identify chromosomally normal embryos before transfer - particularly valuable for couples with a history of recurrent miscarriage or known genetic conditions.

The IVF Process at Kulki IVF - Best IVF Hospital Serving Dausa

For patients travelling from Dausa to our best IVF hospital in Jaipur, here is a clear overview of the treatment journey:

  1. Initial Consultation: A detailed meeting with our fertility specialist to review medical history, perform initial tests, and discuss your personalized IVF plan.
  2. Baseline Investigations: Blood tests (hormonal panel, AMH), pelvic ultrasound, and semen analysis form the foundation of your diagnostic workup.
  3. Ovarian Stimulation (10-14 days): Daily hormone injections to stimulate follicle growth, monitored with serial ultrasounds (most monitoring visits can be spaced to minimize travel for Dausa patients).
  4. Egg Retrieval Day: A single visit to our Jaipur clinic for the egg retrieval procedure under sedation.
  5. Fertilization & Embryo Culture (5 days): Your embryos are cultured in our world-class laboratory. You do not need to visit during this phase.
  6. Embryo Transfer: A quick, painless visit to transfer the selected embryo into the uterus.
  7. Pregnancy Test (Day 14): A blood beta-hCG test confirms your result. Many patients can have this done locally in Dausa and share results with our team digitally.

Our team actively minimizes unnecessary visits to Jaipur, making our best IVF hospital genuinely accessible for Dausa patients.

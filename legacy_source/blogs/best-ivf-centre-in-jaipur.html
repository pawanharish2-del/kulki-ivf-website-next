Frequently Asked Questions (IVF Insights)

Why is blastocyst culture recommended?

Blastocyst culture involves growing embryos to the Day-5 stage. This extended development helps our embryology team identify the most robust, highly viable embryos for transfer, significantly increasing cycle success rates.

What causes IVF implantation failure?

Implantation failure can stem from varied factors, including embryo genetic anomalies, thin endometrial linings, or sub-optimal lab environments. Our team performs detailed uterine mapping and laser hatching to address these issues in repeated cycle failures.

Is IVF treatment painful?

Most patients experience only mild discomfort during hormone injections. The egg retrieval procedure itself is performed under light anesthesia, meaning you will feel no pain during the process.
